World & Biomes

  • Surface Grass, trees, and early mobs. Start here.
  • Underground Stone caves with coal, iron, gold ores.
  • Deep Titanium ore and copper — rare and valuable.
  • Lava Layer Extreme heat zone. Requires fire resistance potion!
  • Dungeon Gargoyle bosses guard rare chests.
  • Rocket Pad Your launchpad — the final goal!
🏗️

Progression Path

  1. Collect Wood & Stone → Craft Workbench
  2. Smelt ores in the Furnace
  3. Fight Gargoyle in dungeons for rare loot
  4. Craft Abyssal Sigil → Summon the final boss
  5. Defeat Abyssal Behemoth for Quantum Engine
  6. Craft the Rocket and launch to space! 🚀
🌲

Platforms

  • 🪵 Wooden Platforms are one-way floors
  • Jump up through them from below
  • Press S while standing on them to drop down
  • Great for building boss arenas!
  • Recipe: 1 Wood → 4 Platforms

⚗️ Alchemy

Use a Brewing Stand to create powerful potions. Place ingredients in the slots and press Brew.

💪 Strength Potion

Doubles your attack damage for a limited time. Great for boss fights!

Ingredients: Slimeball + Coal

🔥 Fire Resistance Potion

Grants immunity to lava damage. Required to explore the lava layer safely!

Ingredients: Magma Clot + Gold Ingot

🌿 Jump Boost Potion

Massively increases jump height. Useful for reaching high areas or escaping enemies.

Ingredients: Slimeball + Bat Wing

💀 Bosses

🗿

Gargoyle

❤️ 500 HP 📍 Dungeons 🔑 Auto-Spawned

Description

Stone gargoyles guard underground dungeons. They patrol their territory and fly toward the player when provoked. Defeat them to access rare chest loot.

Attacks

  • 🪂 Fly Dive — charges toward the player at high speed
  • 👊 Melee Strike — deals contact damage on collision

Loot

💎 Ruby 🔵 Titanium Ingot ⚡ Rare Chest (nearby)
😈

Abyssal Behemoth

❤️ 2000 HP ⚡ 40 Contact DMG ☄️ 15 Orb DMG 🔮 Abyssal Sigil

Description

The final guardian of the world. A massive dark sphere crackling with abyssal energy — glowing red eyes, runic cracks running across its surface, and deadly spikes on all sides. It guards the Quantum Engine, the last piece you need to build your rocket.

🔮 Summon by using the Abyssal Sigil (infinite use — keep it forever!). Build an arena with wooden platforms before summoning!

Attacks

  • 🦘 Jump Slam — launches itself at you with high speed, deals massive contact damage on landing
  • ☄️ Abyssal Volley — fires 3 purple-red orbs in a spread toward you. Dodge sideways!
  • 💥 Attacks alternate randomly — never stand still!

💡 Tips

  • Build a platform arena — stay mobile above and below
  • Drink a Strength Potion before the fight
  • Use the Bow from a safe distance to avoid orbs
  • The boss flashes when hit — you can track damage visually

Loot

🔬 Quantum Engine (100%) 💎 Ruby ×15 🔵 Titanium Ingot ×15 ⚡ OVERPOWERED SWORD 666 (5%)

🚀 Progression Guide

1

Early Game — Surface Survival

Punch trees for wood, craft a Workbench and Furnace. Mine coal and iron. Build a shelter before night — Zombie Cats will come!

Goal: Iron Pickaxe & Sword, Furnace built
2

Mid Game — Underground Exploration

Dig deep for gold and titanium. Hunt Bats for Bat Wings (Bow & Hook). Build ladders as you descend for easy travel back up.

Goal: Titanium gear, Grappling Hook, Bow
3

Lava Layer — Fire & Fury

Brew a Fire Resistance Potion first! Fish with the Lava Fishing Rod to collect Magma Clots. Craft the Lavacalibur here.

Goal: Ruby collection, Lavacalibur
4

Boss Fights — Prove Your Worth

Collect Zombie Brains at night. Craft the Abyssal Sigil. Build a platform arena and summon the Abyssal Behemoth. Stay mobile and dodge the orbs!

Goal: Quantum Engine from boss drop
🚀

Endgame — Build the Rocket

With the Quantum Engine, craft the Rocket Turbine. Assemble all 3 rocket parts and place them on the Rocket Pad. Launch to victory!

🏆 You WIN! Paws & Pickaxes completed!
